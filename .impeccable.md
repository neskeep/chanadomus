## Design Context

### Users
- **Primary**: 86 propietarios de condominio residencial (Ranchos de Chana, Venezuela). Mayoría personas mayores, no técnicas. Usan la app en móvil para gestionar visitas, ver estado de cuenta, reportar incidencias, votar, y comunicarse con la comunidad.
- **Secondary**: Administrador (1-2 personas, desktop/tablet), Vigilancia (móvil/tablet en caseta), Conserje (tablet).
- **Context**: PWA mobile-first. Los propietarios la usan desde casa o en movimiento. Vigilancia la usa en turnos de 12h. Admin la usa para gestión diaria.
- **Accessibility**: Textos legibles para personas mayores — mínimo 16px body, 18-20px preferido. Contraste alto. Touch targets generosos (48px+). Sin gestos complejos.

### Brand Personality
- **3 words**: Confiable, moderna, accesible
- **Voice**: Profesional pero cercana. Nunca fría ni corporativa. Nunca infantil.
- **Emotional goal**: Simplicidad y control — "tengo todo a la mano sin complicaciones"
- **Tone**: Claro, directo, amable. Español neutro latinoamericano.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone**: Clean SaaS property management — spacious layouts, clear hierarchy, soft shadows, sophisticated color palette with defined accent. Think modern fintech/proptech adapted for older, non-technical users.
- **Reference** (HOMEAGENT Dashboard — Dribbble):
  - Clean white background with green accent palette (aligns with our teal primary)
  - Top metric cards: icon + large bold number + trend indicator + subtle progress bar — no heavy shadows
  - Cards with thin borders and rounded corners, no aggressive drop shadows
  - Typography: titles semi-bold, data numbers extra-bold/large, labels in regular/muted weight
  - Charts (bar, line, donut) using brand colors, clean axes, minimal gridlines
  - Grid: 4-col metrics → 3-col charts → 2-col bottom (content + chat sidebar)
  - Generous spacing between all sections and cards
  - Navigation: pill-shaped active state, clean horizontal nav with search/notifications
  - **Adapt to mobile**: This is desktop — our mobile version should preserve the hierarchy and spacing but stack to single column with bottom nav
- **Anti-references**: 
  - NO "AI generated by default" aesthetic — no generic card grids, no gradient text, no glassmorphism, no hero metric layouts
  - NO junior-level design — no flat/boring layouts, no cramped spacing, no weak hierarchy
  - NO government/institutional feel — no gray/dull interfaces
  - NO overly corporate/cold aesthetic
- **Theme**: Light mode primary (elderly users, daytime use). Dark mode optional/secondary.
- **Typography**: Legible, warm, professional. NOT Inter (current, too generic). Larger sizes for elderly audience. Clear weight hierarchy.

### Design Principles
1. **Clarity over cleverness** — Every element must be immediately understandable. If a 65-year-old can't figure it out in 3 seconds, it's too complex.
2. **Generous space** — Breathing room everywhere. No cramped layouts. Touch-friendly spacing that doubles as visual comfort.
3. **Intentional hierarchy** — One primary action per screen. Clear visual weight tells users exactly where to look and what to do.
4. **Warm professionalism** — The app should feel like a well-run building lobby: organized, welcoming, trustworthy. Not a hospital, not a playground.
5. **Progressive disclosure** — Show what matters now. Details available on demand. Never overwhelm.

### Card & Feed Standards (validated in vigilancia/accesos.vue)

**Card compacto (2 filas max)**:
- Row 1: Contenido principal (nombre/titulo text-sm font-semibold) + badges + hora
- Row 2: Meta inline text-[11px] con `·` separators + accion ghost inline al final
- Padding: `px-3 py-2.5`, spacing: `space-y-2`
- SIN bordes coloreados, SIN fondos de estado — cards limpios siempre
- Acciones SIEMPRE inline (ghost h-6) — NUNCA en fila propia

**Timeline (feeds cronologicos)**:
- Linea vertical `w-px bg-border` + dots `size-2.5` coloreados por estado
- Dots: primary=ok, destructive=error, amber-500=warning
- Container `pl-5`, dot `absolute -left-5`

**Cuando una accion se completa**, el resultado reemplaza el boton en el mismo slot inline (no agrega fila nueva).

**Anti-patterns (rechazados por el usuario)**:
- NO bordes izquierdos coloreados en cards
- NO fondos coloreados por estado en cards
- NO dots grandes con ring grueso (ring-4)
- NO botones de accion en fila separada debajo del contenido
- NO timeline dots con iconos dentro (CheckCircle, XCircle) — solo dots de color solido
